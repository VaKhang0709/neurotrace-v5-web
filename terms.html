1. Research purpose and eligibility

NeuroTrace V6 is an optional research data-collection interface for adults aged 18 or older. It requests drawing trajectories and self-reported context for research into handwriting and motor signals. It is not a clinical service, medical device, or substitute for professional care.

2. Voluntary use and consent

Participation is voluntary. You must complete the consent checks before using the guided protocol. You may skip tasks, stop, or decline to submit the final questionnaire. You may withdraw future consent; deletion of synchronized research records is handled under the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

3. No medical advice or diagnosis

Model probabilities and classifications are experimental research outputs. They must not be used to diagnose, rule out, monitor, or treat Parkinson's disease or any other condition. Do not change care based on the interface. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns.

4. Accurate and respectful use

Provide only information you are comfortable sharing and do not enter names, addresses, contact information, or third-party personal data. Do not attempt to identify other participants, probe Firebase endpoints, bypass security rules, upload malicious content, or disrupt the service. Only submit your own drawing data.
