Research FAQ

Is this a diagnostic test?

No. This is not a diagnostic tool.

What is collected?

Only consented research data.

Can I skip a drawing?

Yes, tasks can be skipped.

How do I request deletion?

Contact the research project owner.

Is data sent while Firebase is unconfigured?

No, it is queued locally.

Research collection is not initialized.

This research collection requires a modern browser with JavaScript and secure local storage. The V5 demo is still available.

Research transparency and identity controls

The random device ID and browser fingerprint are pseudonymous identifiers, not guaranteed anonymous. Resetting an ID does not delete cloud records or change queued records.
